Painting & Related Home Improvement Services


I'm a local painter providing affordable, reliable painting and related home improvement and repair services. I take pride in delivering quality workmanship, paying attention to the details, and treating every property with care and respect.


Whether you need a room repainted, damaged drywall repaired, old wallpaper removed, or new trim installed, I'm committed to providing results you'll be happy with.


I work in St Mary's, Stratford, London, Kitchener, Waterloo and nearby areas in Southwestern Ontario.

Services

  • Interior painting
  • Exterior painting
  • Drywall & plaster repairs
  • Caulking
  • Staining
  • Baseboard & trim installation
  • Wallpaper application & removal
  • Surface preparation & priming
  • Work at heights

Feel free to contact me with any questions. These are my areas of expertise, but I'm happy to discuss other projects. As a one-person operation, I keep my overhead low — no crew, no subcontractors, no markup — so you get quality work at a fair price. No project is too big or too small. And you don't pay until the job is done and you're happy with it.
